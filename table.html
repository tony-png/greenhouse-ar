How it works

  1. Point the phone at a table and move it slowly until the ring appears.
  2. Tap to place the model (1:20, about 29 cm long). Tap again to move it somewhere else.
  3. Drag to slide it, pinch to resize, twist two fingers to turn it, or use the scale buttons.
  4. Tap Lock to fix it in place, then walk around it. Options switches the look, contact shadows and room reflections; Assembly plays the build step by step.
  5. Gus the gardener works inside and Bolt the robot runs round to whichever side you're on. Tap either of them to talk; press and drag to carry them, flick to throw them. At Life size, Bolt follows you around the yard.
Needs Chrome on an Android phone with ARCore (Google Play Services for AR). Prefer it full size? Try the backyard mode. Photo wood: Poly Haven “Hinoki Planks” (CC0). A planning model, not a structural design.
