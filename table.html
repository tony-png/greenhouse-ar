Greenhouse on your table

The v2 greenhouse (two-ply 2x6 arches) as a scale model in AR. Place it on a table, size it from 1:100 up to life size, turn it, lock it, compare finishes, and play the build step by step. On a Meta Quest you can also walk round it in a virtual backyard.

On a phone

  1. Point the phone at a table and move it slowly until the ring appears.
  2. Tap to place the model (1:20, about 29 cm long). Tap again to move it somewhere else.
  3. Drag to slide it, pinch to resize, twist two fingers to turn it, or use the scale buttons.
  4. Tap Lock to fix it in place, then walk around it. Options switches the look and the extras; Assembly plays the build step by step.
  5. Gus the gardener works inside and Bolt the robot runs round to whichever side you're on. Tap either of them to talk; press and drag to carry them, flick to throw them. At Life size, Bolt follows you around the yard.

On a Meta Quest

  1. Put it on my table shows it in your room (passthrough). Walk round it in VR puts you in a virtual backyard with the greenhouse at life size.
  2. Point a controller (or your hand) at the floor or a table and pull the trigger (or pinch) to place it.
  3. Trigger-drag to slide it. Hold both grips (or pinch with both hands) and pull apart or twist to resize and turn it. The thumbstick also turns and resizes it. B/Y locks it.
  4. Look at your left hand for the menu, or press A/X to pin it in front of you. Press its buttons with the ray.
  5. In VR: left stick to walk, right stick to turn, trigger (or pinch) on the grass to teleport.
  6. Point at Gus or Bolt and pull the trigger to talk; hold to carry them, flick to throw. With a room scan (Space Setup), Bolt walks round your real furniture.
Phones: Chrome on Android with ARCore. Headsets: the Meta Quest browser. Prefer it full size on a phone? Try the backyard mode. Photo wood: Poly Haven “Hinoki Planks” (CC0). A planning model of the v2 design, not a structural drawing.
