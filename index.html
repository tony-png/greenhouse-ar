Greenhouse in your backyard

The wooden lattice-rib tunnel greenhouse at true 1:1 scale. Open this page in Chrome on your phone, then place it on your lawn and walk around it, or inside it.

Tips

  1. Go out in daylight; AR struggles in the dark and on plain, glare-y surfaces.
  2. Before it appears, move the phone slowly and point at the ground so it can find the lawn.
  3. You need a clear area of about 6 × 8 m. The pale rectangle is the 4.7 × 5.9 m gravel pad.
  4. Needs an ARCore phone (most recent Android phones) with Google Play Services for AR.
Shown at true scale and fixed size: pinching won't shrink it in AR. A planning model, not a structural design.
